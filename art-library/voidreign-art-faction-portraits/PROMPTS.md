# VOIDREIGN faction portrait generation brief

Generated 2026-11-08 with the free AI Horde anonymous volunteer pool, model **AlbedoBase XL (SDXL)**. Each source is a 512×512 WebP. The native game portraits are 192×192 PNGs, bundled in `game-data/Pictures/Races/<Faction>/<Faction>_Race_Portrait.png`. Original source images and worker/seed/checksum metadata are kept beside this file.

## Shared art direction

Dark science-fiction strategy game character key art. Centered chest-up portrait, readable face and shoulders, premium painterly realism, cinematic lighting, detailed organic and metallic materials, a dark starship or orbital setting, strong silhouette, square composition. No typography, captions, logos, signatures or frames.
